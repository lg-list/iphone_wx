Privacy Policy

FixMob provides iPhone repair reference pages, model navigation, screw location photos, and safety notes. This policy explains what information may be processed when you browse this website.

Information processed by the site

FixMob does not require account registration and does not ask visitors to submit payment details, passwords, repair history, or device serial numbers. Standard hosting logs may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, referring page, and request time so the site can diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and understand aggregate traffic.

Cookies, advertising, and analytics

FixMob may use Google AdSense or similar advertising services. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, personalize, or limit ads according to their own policies and the visitor's browser or account settings. Visitors can manage cookies in their browser and can review Google's advertising settings from their Google account.

If analytics tools are enabled, they are used to understand aggregate page performance, popular repair topics, broken links, and device/browser compatibility. Analytics are not used to collect repair-shop customer records or sensitive device ownership information.

Affiliate links and external sites

Some repair pages may link to outside resources such as manufacturer information, tool references, replacement parts, or safety documentation. External websites have their own privacy policies and content practices. FixMob is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

Data retention and choices

Hosting and security logs are retained only as long as needed for site reliability, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and ordinary operational review. You can block or delete cookies through your browser. Some site features may load more slowly or display less relevant advertising if cookies are disabled.

For privacy questions, correction requests, or site policy questions, contact FixMob at contact@fixmob.tech.
