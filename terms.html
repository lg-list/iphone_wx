Terms and Repair Disclaimer

FixMob provides general repair information, model navigation, safety notes, screw references, and photo-based repair planning for iPhone devices. The information is provided for educational use.

No professional repair relationship

Using this website does not create a technician-client relationship. Device repair can cause damage, data loss, injury, fire, or loss of water resistance. Visitors are responsible for deciding whether they have the tools, parts, skills, and workspace required for a repair.

Battery and glass safety

Lithium-ion batteries can be hazardous if punctured, bent, crushed, overheated, or improperly disposed of. Broken glass can cause injury. If a repair involves swollen batteries, heat, sharp glass, or unknown damage, consider professional service.

Parts, tools, and compatibility

FixMob may describe common tools, symptoms, screw handling, adhesive steps, and model-specific checks, but it does not sell parts or certify that a specific third-party replacement part will match every regional device variant. Visitors should confirm the exact model, part number, connector layout, and seller policy before buying replacement parts.

Water resistance and device data

Opening an iPhone can reduce or remove water resistance even if new adhesive is installed. Repairs can also create data-loss risk if the device, battery, display, board, or connectors are already damaged. Back up important data before attempting any repair whenever the phone still powers on.
