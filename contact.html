Contact FixMob

Use this page for website feedback, correction requests, privacy questions, and policy questions related to FixMob repair guides.

Email

contact@fixmob.tech

Guide corrections

If a repair page has a model mismatch, unclear wording, broken image, missing screw note, or outdated safety warning, send the exact page URL and the repair category. Helpful reports usually include the iPhone model, whether the page is for screen, battery, or back glass repair, the step number if available, and what part of the page should be reviewed.

Website and indexing feedback

You can also report crawl problems, broken internal links, missing images, sitemap issues, or pages that appear to show the wrong model. FixMob uses this type of feedback to improve the public repair guide library and make the site easier for search engines and visitors to navigate.

What to include

For repair guide corrections, include the page URL, iPhone model, repair type, step number, and a short explanation of the issue. For privacy or policy questions, include the page URL and the request type.

Important repair note

FixMob does not provide emergency repair support, device diagnosis by email, or warranty decisions. If a battery is swollen, punctured, overheating, smoking, or leaking, stop using the device and contact a qualified repair professional or local battery disposal service.

Response expectations

FixMob reviews messages as site feedback and policy requests. The website cannot guarantee an individual repair outcome, part compatibility decision, data recovery result, or same-day troubleshooting response.
