About FixMob

FixMob organizes iPhone repair information by model and repair type so visitors can quickly find screen, battery, back glass, and screw-location references for the exact device they are working on.

The site focuses on practical repair planning: model identification, safety warnings, tool awareness, screw handling, connector risk, and photo-based navigation. The goal is to help beginners understand the sequence of a repair before opening a phone.

Who the guides are for

FixMob is written for English-speaking visitors in the United States who are comparing iPhone repair options, preparing a do-it-yourself repair, checking whether a model uses a different opening path, or trying to understand which screws and connectors require extra attention. Each repair page is organized around a single iPhone model and a single repair task so the page can answer a specific search instead of sending every visitor through one generic template.

How pages are organized

Repair pages are grouped into screen replacement, battery replacement, back glass repair, and screw location photo resources. A page normally includes the model year, screen size, port type, symptoms, part checks, tool emphasis, photo sequence, screw or bracket notes, closing tests, and links to related repair types for the same model.

Editorial approach

FixMob favors clear repair preparation over dramatic claims. Pages are updated when model coverage changes, local photos are added, screw tables are improved, or safety notes need clarification. The site does not promise that every visitor should repair a device personally; some repairs, especially swollen batteries and back glass work involving heat or broken glass, may be better handled by a qualified technician.
