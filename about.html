About FixMob

FixMob organizes iPhone repair information by model and repair type so visitors can quickly find screen, battery, back glass, and screw-location references for the exact device they are working on.

The site focuses on practical repair planning: model identification, safety warnings, tool awareness, screw handling, connector risk, and photo-based navigation. The goal is to help beginners understand the sequence of a repair before opening a phone.

FixMob is an independent repair information website and is not affiliated with Apple Inc. iPhone and Apple are trademarks of Apple Inc.
