Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 11 Pro Max battery job

Chassis profile

The 2019 iPhone 11 Pro Max is a 6.5-inch Lightning model. large triple-camera OLED model with a bigger battery footprint.

Repair path

This page focuses on open the phone safely, disconnect power early, release stretch adhesive, fit the new cell, and verify startup before final sealing. The 43 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, plastic cards ready first, then add isopropyl alcohol, opening picks, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

When this page fits

Use this iPhone 11 Pro Max guide for weak battery health, short runtime, random shutdowns, swelling risk, or charging instability. The photos are arranged as a practical bench sequence so you can stop after each action and compare the part position.

large triple-camera OLED model with a bigger battery footprint. For this battery repair, watch battery puncture, adhesive strips, board connectors, Taptic Engine area, and bracket screw order.

Prepare the workspace

  • Power off the Lightning iPhone and keep the battery below 25% when the battery area will be exposed.
  • Make a screw map for the 6.5-inch housing before removing connector covers.
  • Keep heat gentle, tools shallow, and photos open beside the phone.

Before sealing

Check battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. This final check matters more on iPhone 11 Pro Max because large triple-camera OLED model with a bigger battery footprint.

Model-specific rule: The 11-series frame gives more working room than the mini models, but the display and battery areas still punish mixed screws.

FAQ

iPhone 11 Pro Max battery repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 11 Pro Max has its own 6.5-inch housing, Lightning port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this battery repair, test battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 11 Pro Max repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
