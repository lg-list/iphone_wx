Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone Air battery job

Chassis profile

The 2025 iPhone Air is a 6.5-inch USB-C model. thin single-camera model where flex cable strain and pressure marks need extra care.

Repair path

This page focuses on open the phone safely, disconnect power early, release stretch adhesive, fit the new cell, and verify startup before final sealing. The 29 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, plastic cards ready first, then add isopropyl alcohol, opening picks, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

Step By Step

iPhone Air battery photo sequence

Follow these 29 photos in order. Each iPhone Air step includes a model note, the action from the photo, and a risk check for the battery repair.

01

Prepare your phone for disassembly

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

Let your phone's battery drain below 25% before starting this repair - charged lithium-ion battery may catch fire if damaged. Unplug any cables from your phone. Hold the power and either volume button and slide to power off your phone.

Battery rule: never pry with metal against the cell, and keep alcohol away from powered connectors.

02

Mark your opening picks

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

If inserted too far, an opening pick can damage your device. Follow this step to mark your pick and prevent damage. Measure 3 mm from the tip and mark the opening pick with a permanent marker. You can also mark the other corners of the pick with different measurements. Alternatively, tape a coin to a pick 3 mm from the tip.

Battery rule: never pry with metal against the cell, and keep alcohol away from powered connectors.

03

Cracked glass preparation

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

Glass shards can complicate disassembly - r worse, cause injury. If your phone's back glass is cracked, follow this step. Apply packing tape to the cracked glass until it's completely covered - his will help keep the glass contained and allow the suction cup to stick. Make sure there's a single strip (not overlapping) of tape on the bottom edge, big enough for a suction cup to fit on. Only cover the glass itself - on't stick any tape to the frame....

Battery rule: never pry with metal against the cell, and keep alcohol away from powered connectors.

04

Remove the pentalobe screws

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air screw note: record this location before lifting the part. 6.5-inch models can reuse similar screw colors with different lengths.

Use a P2 pentalobe driver to remove the two 4.9 mm-long screws on either side of the USB-C port.

Screw rule: return each screw to the exact battery step location; a long screw in the wrong hole can damage the board.

05

Heat the bottom edge

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

Apply a heated heat pack to the bottom edge of the back glass for two minutes. Alternatively, you can use a hair dryer or heat gun to heat the bottom edge of the back glass until it's slightly too hot to touch. Improper use of a heat gun can destroy the display and/or battery - ollow the linked instructions carefully.

Adhesive rule: reheat and work shallow. On iPhone Air, forcing a cold edge can bend clips or mark the frame.

06

Insert an opening pick

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

Apply a suction handle to the bottom edge of the back glass, as close to the edge as possible. Pull up on the handle with strong, steady force to create a gap between the back glass and the frame. If the back glass doesn't budge, reheat it and try again. Insert the tip of an opening pick into the gap.

Battery rule: never pry with metal against the cell, and keep alcohol away from powered connectors.

07

Back glass information

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

As you slice the adhesive securing the back glass in the next steps, be careful of the following areas: There's a delicate cable connecting the back glass to the phone, right next to the volume up button. There are multiple spring contacts along the top edge of the phone. Be extra careful not to insert your pick deeper than instructed in each step to avoid bending these contacts.

Battery rule: never pry with metal against the cell, and keep alcohol away from powered connectors.

08

Separate the bottom edge adhesive

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

Slide your pick back and forth along the bottom edge to separate the adhesive. Leave your pick inserted in the bottom left corner to prevent the adhesive from resealing.

Adhesive rule: reheat and work shallow. On iPhone Air, forcing a cold edge can bend clips or mark the frame.

Adhesive rule: reheat and work shallow. On iPhone Air, forcing a cold edge can bend clips or mark the frame.

Adhesive rule: reheat and work shallow. On iPhone Air, forcing a cold edge can bend clips or mark the frame.

Swing open the back glass

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

Don't try to fully remove the back glass just yet - t's still attached with a delicate ribbon cable. If the back glass doesn't swing open easily, don't force it - o back around the perimeter with your pick to separate missed sections of adhesive or stuck clips. Gently lift the back glass and swing it open towards the volume buttons. Support the back glass with a clean, sturdy object like a small box to avoid straining the cable.

15

Protect the rear camera

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air battery note: use plastic tools near the cell and pause if adhesive resistance suddenly increases.

We recommend using polyimide (aka kapton) tape to keep the camera lens clean. If you don't have any, you may skip this step, but be extra careful not to touch the camera. Set a piece of polyimide tape over the rear camera lens. Don't press down on the rear camera. During reassembly, carefully remove the tape without jostling the rear camera - ts stabilizing mechanism is extremely fragile.

Battery rule: never pry with metal against the cell, and keep alcohol away from powered connectors.

Remove the left connector cover

iPhone Air note: iPhone Air screw note: record this location before lifting the part. 6.5-inch models can reuse similar screw colors with different lengths.

Remove the three screws securing the left connector cover, located below the rear camera: One 1.1 mm-long tri-point Y000 screw One 1.0 mm-long tri-point Y000 screw One 3.2 mm-long JIS 00 screw JIS 00 bits are designed for these screws. You can try Phillips drivers, but you risk stripping the screw.

Connector rule: align over the socket first, then press from one side and the other. Do not press the middle if it feels misaligned.

Disconnect the battery

Don't try to force the connector into place. If you're having trouble, reposition it and try again.

FAQ

iPhone Air battery repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone Air has its own 6.5-inch housing, USB-C port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this battery repair, test battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone Air repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
