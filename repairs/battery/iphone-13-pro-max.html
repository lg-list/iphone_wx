Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 13 Pro Max battery job

Chassis profile

The 2021 iPhone 13 Pro Max is a 6.7-inch Lightning model. large ProMotion OLED model with heavy glass and a wide battery area.

Repair path

This page focuses on open the phone safely, disconnect power early, release stretch adhesive, fit the new cell, and verify startup before final sealing. The 38 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, plastic cards ready first, then add isopropyl alcohol, opening picks, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

FAQ

iPhone 13 Pro Max battery repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 13 Pro Max has its own 6.7-inch housing, Lightning port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this battery repair, test battery connector seating, clean adhesive channels, boot behavior, charging response, and no loose screw inside the housing. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 13 Pro Max repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
