Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 16 Pro back glass job

Chassis profile

The 2024 iPhone 16 Pro is a 6.3-inch USB-C model. Pro model with Camera Control hardware and tight display connector routing.

Repair path

This page focuses on control heat, protect cameras and wireless charging parts, manage glass fragments, and reseal the rear assembly cleanly. The 46 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep heat source, eye protection, opening picks ready first, then add plastic cards, tweezers, adhesive strips as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect camera openings, wireless charging area, flash alignment, glass fragment cleanup, and even rear-panel pressure. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

iPhone 16 Pro back glass repair planning notes

When this page fits

Use this iPhone 16 Pro guide for shattered rear glass, sharp cracks, damaged camera surround, wireless charging problems, or rear housing repair planning. The photos are arranged as a practical bench sequence so you can stop after each action and compare the part position.

Main risk on this model

Pro model with Camera Control hardware and tight display connector routing. For this back glass repair, watch broken glass, camera lenses, wireless charging coil, flash cable, battery heat exposure, and rear adhesive alignment.

Prepare the workspace

  • Power off the USB-C iPhone and keep the battery below 25% when the battery area will be exposed.
  • Make a screw map for the 6.3-inch housing before removing connector covers.
  • Keep heat gentle, tools shallow, and photos open beside the phone.

Before sealing

Check camera openings, wireless charging area, flash alignment, glass fragment cleanup, and even rear-panel pressure. This final check matters more on iPhone 16 Pro because Pro model with Camera Control hardware and tight display connector routing.

FAQ

iPhone 16 Pro back glass repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 16 Pro has its own 6.3-inch housing, USB-C port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this back glass repair, test camera openings, wireless charging area, flash alignment, glass fragment cleanup, and even rear-panel pressure. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 16 Pro repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
