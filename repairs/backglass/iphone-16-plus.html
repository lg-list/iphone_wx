What is different about the iPhone 16 Plus back glass job

Chassis profile

The 2024 iPhone 16 Plus is a 6.7-inch USB-C model. larger vertical-camera model with a wide display and longer adhesive perimeter.

Repair path

This page focuses on control heat, protect cameras and wireless charging parts, manage glass fragments, and reseal the rear assembly cleanly. The 44 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

FAQ

iPhone 16 Plus back glass repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 16 Plus has its own 6.7-inch housing, USB-C port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this back glass repair, test camera openings, wireless charging area, flash alignment, glass fragment cleanup, and even rear-panel pressure. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 16 Plus repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
