Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone XS screen job

Chassis profile

The 2018 iPhone XS is a 5.8-inch Lightning model. compact OLED model with a dense internal layout around the display connectors.

Repair path

This page focuses on separate the display adhesive, protect display and sensor flex cables, move any required small parts, and test the panel before sealing. The 32 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, suction handle ready first, then add opening picks, heat source, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

Handle the pentalobe screws

iPhone XS note: iPhone XS screw note: record this location before lifting the part. 5.8-inch models can reuse similar screw colors with different lengths.

Before you begin, discharge your iPhone battery below 25%. A charged lithium-ion battery can catch fire and/or explode if accidentally punctured. Power off your iPhone before beginning disassembly. Remove the two 6.9 mm-long pentalobe screws at the bottom edge of the iPhone. Opening the iPhone's display will compromise its waterproof seals. Have replacement seals ready before you proceed past this step, or take care to avoid liquid exposure if you reassemble your iPhone without replacing the seals.

Screw rule: return each screw to the exact screen step location; a long screw in the wrong hole can damage the board.

FAQ

iPhone XS screen repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone XS has its own 5.8-inch housing, Lightning port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this screen repair, test touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone XS repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
