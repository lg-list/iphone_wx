Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 12 Pro screen job

Chassis profile

The 2020 iPhone 12 Pro is a 6.1-inch Lightning model. Flat-sided Pro model with camera and LiDAR hardware close to the rear assembly.

Repair path

This page focuses on how to separate the display adhesive, protect display and sensor flex cables, move any required small parts, and test the panel before sealing. The 33 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, suction handle ready first, then add opening picks, heat source, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

FAQ

iPhone 12 Pro screen repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 12 Pro has its own 6.1-inch housing, Lightning port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this screen repair, test touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 12 Pro repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
