FAQ

iPhone 15 Pro Max screen repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 15 Pro Max has its own 6.7-inch housing, USB-C port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this screen repair, test touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 15 Pro Max repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
