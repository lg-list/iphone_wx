Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 15 Pro screen job

Chassis profile

The 2023 iPhone 15 Pro is a 6.1-inch USB-C model. titanium Pro model where camera, display, and rear glass parts sit tightly together.

Repair path

This page focuses on separate the display adhesive, protect display and sensor flex cables, move any required small parts, and test the panel before sealing. The 59 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, suction handle ready first, then add opening picks, heat source, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

When this page fits

Use this iPhone 15 Pro guide for cracked glass, black display, green lines, ghost touch, or failed touch response. The photos are arranged as a practical bench sequence so you can stop after each action and compare the part position.

titanium Pro model where camera, display, and rear glass parts sit tightly together. For this screen repair, watch display flex cables, top sensor hardware, waterproof adhesive, and mixed-length bracket screws.

Prepare the workspace

  • Power off the USB-C iPhone and keep the battery below 25% when the battery area will be exposed.

Before sealing

Model-specific rule: The 15 Pro titanium frame and camera area leave less margin for aggressive tools near the rear assembly.

FAQ

iPhone 15 Pro screen repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 15 Pro has its own 6.1-inch housing, USB-C port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this screen repair, test touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 15 Pro repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
