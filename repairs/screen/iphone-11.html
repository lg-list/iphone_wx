Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 11 screen job

Chassis profile

The 2019 iPhone 11 is a 6.1-inch Lightning model. dual-camera LCD model with a tall battery and broad adhesive perimeter.

Repair path

This page focuses on separate the display adhesive, protect display and sensor flex cables, move any required small parts, and test the panel before sealing. The 33 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, suction handle ready first, then add opening picks, heat source, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

iPhone 11 screen repair planning notes

When this page fits

Use this iPhone 11 guide for cracked glass, black display, green lines, ghost touch, or failed touch response. The photos are arranged as a practical bench sequence so you can stop after each action and compare the part position.

Main risk on this model

dual-camera LCD model with a tall battery and broad adhesive perimeter. For this screen repair, watch display flex cables, top sensor hardware, waterproof adhesive, and mixed-length bracket screws.

Prepare the workspace

  • Power off the Lightning iPhone and keep the battery below 25% when the battery area will be exposed.
  • Make a screw map for the 6.1-inch housing before removing connector covers.
  • Keep heat gentle, tools shallow, and photos open beside the phone.

Before sealing

Check touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. This final check matters more on iPhone 11 because dual-camera LCD model with a tall battery and broad adhesive perimeter.

Model-specific rule: The 11-series frame gives more working room than the mini models, but the display and battery areas still punish mixed screws.

iPhone 11 screen photo sequence

01

Remove the pentalobe screws

iPhone 11 note: iPhone 11 screw note: record this location before lifting the part. 6.1-inch models can reuse similar screw colors with different lengths.

Before you begin, discharge your iPhone battery below 25%. A charged lithium-ion battery can catch fire and/or explode if accidentally punctured. Power off your iPhone before beginning disassembly. Remove the two 6.7 mm-long pentalobe P2 screws at the bottom edge of the iPhone. Opening the iPhone's display will compromise its waterproof seals. Have replacement seals ready before you proceed past this step, or take care to avoid liquid exposure if you reassemble your iPhone without replacing the seals.

Screw rule: return each screw to the exact screen step location; a long screw in the wrong hole can damage the board.

02

Mark your opening picks

iPhone 11 note: iPhone 11 display note: support the panel at a shallow angle so the 2019 flex routing is not pulled.

If inserted too far, an opening pick can damage your device. Follow this step to mark your pick and prevent damage. Measure 3 mm from the tip and mark the opening pick with a permanent marker. You can also mark the other corners of the pick with different measurements. Alternatively, tape a coin to a pick 3 mm from the tip.

Part rule: keep the removed part, bracket, and screws together until the matching reassembly step.

FAQ

iPhone 11 screen repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 11 has its own 6.1-inch housing, Lightning port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this screen repair, test touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 11 repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
