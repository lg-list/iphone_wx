Model Notes

What is different about the iPhone 11 Pro screen job

Chassis profile

The 2019 iPhone 11 Pro is a 5.8-inch Lightning model. triple-camera OLED model with tight upper-board connector spacing.

Repair path

This page focuses on separate the display adhesive, protect display and sensor flex cables, move any required small parts, and test the panel before sealing. The 32 photo steps are kept in bench order so the screw table and the visible part position stay aligned.

Tool emphasis

Keep P2 pentalobe driver, Y000 driver, suction handle ready first, then add opening picks, heat source, spudger as the phone opens.

Quality check

After the repair, inspect touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. If anything fails, reopen before adhesive pressure makes the correction harder.

FAQ

iPhone 11 Pro screen repair questions

Can I use a guide from another iPhone?

No. Similar generations often share repair ideas, but iPhone 11 Pro has its own 5.8-inch housing, Lightning port layout, screw positions, and cable routing.

What should I test before closing?

For this screen repair, test touch response, brightness, True Tone where available, Face ID or sensor behavior, earpiece sound, and clean frame seating. Do this before final adhesive pressure.

Why use the screw table?

The table highlights steps where length or bracket position matters. Mixed screws are one of the easiest ways to turn a simple iPhone 11 Pro repair into board damage.

When should I stop?

Stop if heat becomes excessive, a connector will not align, the battery bends, or glass fragments move toward cameras, cables, or the board.
